\begin{frame}{Business model and commercialisation}
  \footnotesize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.1}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.1cm} X >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.4cm}@{}}
    \toprule
    Segment & Offer & Revenue \\
    \midrule
    Residents and tourists & the app, needs profile, assistant, barrier map & free, always -- they create the data \\
    Venue owners (hotels, food \& drink, culture, chains) & basic panel free; \textbf{Pro plan}: owner badge,
      statistics on “what guests look for and what is missing”, bulk facilities, an accessibility widget
      for the website and the booking system & subscription per venue or a package for chains \\
    Event organisers, property managers & time-limited package: temporary entrances and routes,
      a barrier map for the event & fee per event / venue \\
    Cities and regions & white-label deployment (configuration, OSM import, city layers); reports on
      gaps in data and facilities & deployment + annual maintenance \\
    Maps, travel apps, booking systems & Open API with attribution: accessibility facts with source,
      date and status & API licence / request limits \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}

  \vspace{1mm}
  \textbf{Principles that protect the value of the data.} We do not sell personal data; community content and OSM data
  stay open (ODbL). Owners pay for tools and visibility, not for a “better” status -- the status always follows verification.

  \note[item]{The most realistic first revenue: hotels and medical tourism (the “After treatment” profile, the A\&E / pharmacies layer, 4~languages); guests from abroad ask about accessibility before booking.}
  \note[item]{The owner panel already shows “the most searched-for information about your venue”; that is the seed of the Pro plan statistics.}
  \note[item]{Event package: the owner panel already has bulk-added points inside a venue (lifts, toilets, entrances); an organiser adds them for the duration of the event.}
  \note[item]{The city is not the first customer; it uses the data and the reports, and may fund a white-label deployment.}
\end{frame}
