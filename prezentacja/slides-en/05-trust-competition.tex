  \vspace{1.5mm}
  \begin{block}{Trust model: status, source and date on every fact}
    \scriptsize
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Four sources:} OpenStreetMap, Kraków open data, the owner, the community; the import never overwrites data entered by people.
      \item \textbf{Status computed on every read:}
            \pill{accNavy}{Confirmed by the owner} $>$ \pill{accOk}{Confirmed by the community ($\geq$ 2)}
            $>$ \pill{accNavy}{From an official source} $>$ \pill{accMuted}{Not verified};
            a dispute or 365~days without confirmation $\rightarrow$ \pill{accPartial}{Needs re-checking}.
      \item \textbf{Missing information never means “accessible”;} conflicts are flagged, not averaged; every change stays
            in the history; outages expire after 14~days.
      \item \textbf{When a source is silent:} cache $\rightarrow$ snapshot with an “as of” date; no screen is ever empty.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
