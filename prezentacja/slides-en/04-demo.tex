  \note[item]{Time: about 3 minutes. Before going on stage: the app on a phone and a laptop, the map on Kazimierz, the preferences step-free, accessible toilet and disabled parking ticked in the Profile (not the “Wheelchair” chip: it also requires door width, which OSM data lacks, so it would show “Meets 2 of your 3 needs”), the owner panel open in a second tab.}
  \note[item]{Step 1 (0:00–0:30): show Profile $\rightarrow$ preferences and the “Nearest to the map centre” list; places with the “Matches your needs” badge rank higher.}
  \note[item]{Step 2 (0:30–1:00): Places tab, type “A restaurant in Kazimierz without steps, with an accessible toilet and parking”; in keyword mode the result is deterministic: “I found 535 matching places; 1 of those shown meet all your requirements”, Kuchnia u~Doroty on top.}
  \note[item]{Step 3 (1:00–1:45): open the card; the green box; point at three things next to the attribute: source, date, status. Scroll to an attribute without data (e.g. wide doors): our most important message, we do not guess. Buttons Up to date / Outdated / Correct, “Add” and a question to the owner.}
  \note[item]{Step 4 (1:45–2:20): “Report a problem”, pin at the Stradom stop, type “No lift”, severity “Blocks”; show the marker shape (square = blocks) and the votes. A report needs no account.}
  \note[item]{Step 5 (2:20–3:00): in the second tab, \texttt{/owner.html} as \texttt{kasia@accessly.test} (Camelot Cafe), click “Potwierdź informacje” (the owner panel is in Polish, for venue staff; say what the button does: confirm the information); back on that venue's card the status has changed.}
  \note[item]{Edge cases (if the jury asks or time is left): the “Wheelchair” chip and “Meets 2 of your 3 needs, no data: wide doors”; attribute history with conflicting votes; the “Data sources” page with snapshot dates.}
  \note[item]{If the network fails: everything except the map tiles works from local data. Sample data: three demo accounts and test reports; the admin view without sign-in carries a “Demo mode: sample data” label.}
